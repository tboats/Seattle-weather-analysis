💡 Physical Context & Insights

Why is Winter Warming Strongest?

Seattle winter temperatures showed an exceptionally strong warming anomaly (+3.66°F Lows, +2.61°F Highs). Southerly Pacific storm tracks and marine cloud cover prevent radiational cooling during long winter nights.

Precipitation Volatility Insight

Seattle rainfall was 32.8% more volatile than typical climate variation (σ = 0.235 in/d vs 0.177 in/d). The year featured 18.9% more heavy downpour days (≥0.50 in) separated by longer dry spells.
